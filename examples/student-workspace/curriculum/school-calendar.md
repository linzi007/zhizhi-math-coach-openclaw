# School Calendar

## Defaults

- School year: 2025-2026
- School entry year: 2025
- First semester: 2025-09-01 to 2026-01-31
- Second semester: 2026-02-01 to 2026-08-31
- Current sample date: 2026-05-18
- Current inferred grade/semester: 一年级下学期

## Local Overrides

- Midterm window: configure in the private workspace.
- Final window: configure in the private workspace.
- Winter break: configure in the private workspace.
- Summer break: configure in the private workspace.

## Phase Rules

- Normal semester: use current school progress.
- Final window: prioritize all-semester mistake review.
- Winter break: repair first-semester weak points.
- Summer break: review the whole school year and lightly preview next grade.
